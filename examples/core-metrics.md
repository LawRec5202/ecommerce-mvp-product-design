# Core Metrics for E-commerce MVP

This file lists the key metrics that should be monitored after the e-commerce MVP system is launched.

## 1. Product Detail Page Views

Measures how many users view product detail pages.

Purpose:

- Understand product exposure.
- Analyze whether users are interested in viewing product details.
- Identify products with low traffic.

## 2. Add-to-Cart Rate

Formula:

Add-to-Cart Rate = Add-to-Cart Users / Product Detail Page Visitors

Purpose:

- Measure product attractiveness.
- Understand whether users show purchase intent after viewing product details.
- Identify problems in product information, price, images, or SKU design.

## 3. Order Conversion Rate

Formula:

Order Conversion Rate = Submitted Order Users / Product Detail Page Visitors

Purpose:

- Measure how effectively users move from browsing to order submission.
- Identify friction in the shopping cart or checkout process.
- Evaluate whether the order submission flow is clear and smooth.

## 4. Payment Success Rate

Formula:

Payment Success Rate = Paid Orders / Submitted Orders

Purpose:

- Measure whether the payment process works properly.
- Identify payment failures, timeout issues, or payment gateway problems.
- Ensure the core transaction flow can be completed.

## 5. GMV

Formula:

GMV = Total Amount of Paid Orders

Purpose:

- Measure the overall transaction scale.
- Track sales performance.
- Understand business growth after launch.

## 6. Order Completion Rate

Formula:

Order Completion Rate = Completed Orders / Paid Orders

Purpose:

- Measure whether paid orders are successfully fulfilled.
- Identify issues in shipment, logistics, or order processing.
- Evaluate whether the transaction loop is fully completed.

## 7. Cancellation Rate

Formula:

Cancellation Rate = Cancelled Orders / Submitted Orders

Purpose:

- Identify issues such as payment timeout, inventory problems, or user hesitation.
- Analyze why users cancel orders before completing payment.
- Improve checkout and payment experience.

## 8. Refund Rate

Formula:

Refund Rate = Refunded Orders / Paid Orders

Purpose:

- Measure product quality and after-sales experience.
- Identify products with high refund risk.
- Improve product descriptions, SKU information, logistics, and customer service.
